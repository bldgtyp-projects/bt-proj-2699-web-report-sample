- Note: 16" wood I-joist framing assumed for insulation thickness. Roof framing to be determined by the structural
  engineer or architect.
- Provide a minimum 1" air space above the roof sheathing, connected to vent openings at the eaves and ridge.
- Insulate roof framing with dense-packed cellulose or dense-packed wood-fiber insulation.
- Provide a vapor-variable air barrier / vapor-retarding layer ([Pro Clima Intello
  Plus](https://475.supply/products/intello-plus) or similar) at the interior side of the roof framing. No penetrations
  in this layer.
- Recommended: Add a 1-1/2" to 2-1/2" service cavity inboard of the roof framing for running all plumbing and
  electrical. This helps ensure the continuity and integrity of the interior airtightness layer.
- Use 1/2" GWB or similar on the interior.
