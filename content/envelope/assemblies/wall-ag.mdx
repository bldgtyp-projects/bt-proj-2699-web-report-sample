- Note: 2x6 framed wall assumed for insulation thickness. Wall framing to be determined by the structural engineer or
  architect.
- Insulate framed wall with mineral wool batts.
- Provide sheathing per structural requirements.
- Provide an air barrier at the exterior sheathing surface using either an integrated sheathing/WRB product (Zip System
  or similar) or a separate vapor-permeable air barrier/WRB membrane.
- Install 4" wood-fiber insulation board over the prepared sheathing layer.
- Provide a vented siding assembly over the wood-fiber insulation.
- Recommended: Add a 1-1/2" to 2-1/2" service cavity inboard of the wall framing for running all plumbing and
  electrical. The service cavity may be insulated with typical batt insulation for additional R-value.
- Use 1/2" GWB or similar on the interior with typical latex paint (Class III vapor retarder).
