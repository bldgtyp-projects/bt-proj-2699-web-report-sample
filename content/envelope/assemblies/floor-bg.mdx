- Note: Concrete floor shown for illustration only. Concrete thickness to be determined by the structural engineer or
  architect.
- Target 6" sub-slab XPS foam board (or [Graphite EPS](https://neopor-insulation.com/)) below concrete.
- Sub-slab air and vapor barrier to be installed directly above insulation. Use [15 Mil
  StegoHome](https://475.supply/products/stegohome-15-mil-vapor-barrier) or similar.
- Tape / seal all penetrations and seams of the sub-slab air and vapor barrier.
