- In general, the significant tree shading helps manage overheating risk, and roof overhangs shade the tops of the
  south-facing windows.
- The west-facing windows will receive significant late-day sun, which could lead to overheating. Consider deciduous
  trees or other vegetation such as vines or trellis elements to shade any west-facing units. This type of shading can
  be particularly effective with low-angle sun such as is experienced in the evenings on the west.
