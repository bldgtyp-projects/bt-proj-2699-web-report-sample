The energy balance of the windows is critical on any high-performance building project. The goal for this climate should
be to take full advantage of wintertime solar gains in order to reduce overall heating energy need, while always being
cautious about the potential for overheating and increasing cooling energy need. This can be particularly challenging
with highly glazed rooms or spaces which feature large amounts of south- or west-facing glass.

All radiation values presented consider the local shading context. Where relevant, this context is created using
satellite images and plot lines from [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/) and
[CadMapper](https://cadmapper.com/). For this home, the surrounding mature trees provide the most significant shading.
