Taking into account the climate, orientation, and shading, in the results below we have assessed the average seasonal
(winter / summer) solar radiation falling upon the windows in the project. The radiation levels will vary by orientation
and as can be observed, shading obstructions also have a strong effect on the final level of solar radiation any
individual window receives.

- Good wintertime radiation is observed on the west- and south-facing windows. Solar gains are fairly even over the main
  living areas of the lower level, though some wintertime overheating could occur in the smaller rooms (e.g., the main
  bath).
- Consider operable interior blinds / shades, particularly on the west-facing windows, as winter sun angles will be
  quite low and could cause glare issues.
- None of the north- or east-facing windows provide useful solar gains, and they are thus net energy losers in the
  heating season. Any reduction in glazing on these facades would have a beneficial impact on the annual heating demand.
