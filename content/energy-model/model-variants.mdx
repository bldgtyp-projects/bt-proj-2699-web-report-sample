As shown in the results below, in order to assess the building performance we have tested the building in five distinct
configurations (variants):

1. **Code-Minimum:** A version which meets but does not exceed the **<VarLink
hrefKey="narrative.energy_code.code_base_url" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><Var
k="narrative.energy_code.name" /> / <Var k="narrative.energy_code.zone" /></VarLink>** code minimums for assembly
R-values, window U-values, airtightness and equipment efficiencies. This variant assumes a typical residential
"extract-only" (bath fan) ventilation system and all-electric heat-pump heating, cooling, and hot water.

2. **Insulation:** A variant which increases assembly R-values to levels in line with typical PHI Low Energy Building
projects in this climate zone. For more information, see the [building envelope](/building_envelope/#recommended-assemblies)
section.

3. **Airtightness & ERV:** A variant which meets the PHI Low Energy Building criteria for airtightness (1.0 ACH@50Pa).
For additional information, see the [airtightness section](/building_envelope/#building-airtightness). This version
also replaces the exhaust-only ventilation system of the Code-Minimum version with a balanced fresh-air ventilation
system with heat and moisture recovery (ERV).

4. **Windows:** A variant which upgrades the windows and doors to meet the performance and comfort criteria of the PHI
certification. More information can be found in the [windows section](/windows/#window-products).

5. **As-Built [RECOMMENDED]:** A version which represents our understanding of the building as currently designed,
including minor changes to the wall assembly, improved airtightness (0.6 ACH@50Pa), a reduced thermal-bridge allowance,
and the addition of shading elements.

**Envelope R-values:** Effective R-values (including repeating thermal bridges) are shown. Code-Minimum values are taken
from the <VarLink hrefKey="narrative.energy_code.u_val_link" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><Var
k="narrative.energy_code.name" /> minimum compliance values</VarLink>.

**Thermal bridge allowance:** This is a percentage increase in the overall heat loss of the building. Instead of
performing detailed thermal-bridge calculations, this value is based on past experience and should be understood as a
rough estimate or allowance.
