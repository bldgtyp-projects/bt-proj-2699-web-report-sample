This report evaluates the energy, comfort, and durability performance of the home under various configurations. The
findings suggest significant potential for enhancing the building's energy efficiency beyond the code-minimum standards
set by the local building requirements.

The proposed enhancements outlined in this report would enable the home to far exceed the code-minimum requirements for
building performance, comfort, and durability. We have included an "As-Built" variant to illustrate the performance of
the home as currently designed, benchmarked against the PHI Low Energy Building standard. This standard serves as a
valuable framework for assessing the building's performance even without seeking certification.

We have outlined the details of this assessment modeling, the key results, and our recommendations within the following
pages.

### Primary recommendations include:

- Insulate the walls to approximately R-37, the floor to R-30, and the roof to R-55. For more information and
  recommended assemblies and products, see the [building envelope section](/building_envelope/#recommended-assemblies).
- Use a high-performance window system with triple-pane glazing and thermally broken frames. See the [windows
  section](/windows/#window-products) for details on suitable products.
- Implement airtightness solutions on all walls, ceilings, and floors. For more information, see the [airtightness
  section](/building_envelope/#building-airtightness).
- Use a high-performance dedicated fresh-air ventilation system with heat and moisture recovery (ERV). For more
  information, see the [ventilation section](/mechanical/#fresh-air-ventilation). Keep all ERV ducting inside the
  conditioned envelope and locate the fresh-air intake away from any potential sources of contamination.
- Electrify all home mechanical systems, including heating, cooling, and domestic hot water.
